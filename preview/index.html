Questions

Good to know.

What is BitRep?

A workout tracker for iPhone. It keeps your lifting, cardio, food and steps in one place, and a pixel companion grows as you train.

Is it free?

Yes. Logging your workouts is free forever, and there are no ads.

Can I bring my history from Hevy?

Yes. You can import your Hevy history when you set up BitRep.

When does it launch?

We're finishing it now. Join the waitlist and you'll hear about early access before anyone else.

Is there an Android version?

iPhone comes first. Android is planned.

I'm new to the gym. Is it for me?

Yes. Answer five questions and BitRep suggests a plan, and nothing in the app makes you feel bad for a rest day.

What do I get for joining the waitlist?

First word on early access, and one email on launch day. That's all we'll send.

What happens to my data?

Your workouts live on your phone. We never sell your data.
