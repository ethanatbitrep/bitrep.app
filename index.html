Questions people ask

Do I need an account? No. BitRep works fully offline and sends us nothing until you choose to create one for backup.
Can I bring my history? Yes — import a CSV export from your old app, or start clean. Setup takes under a minute.
What happens if Pro lapses? Nothing is deleted. Your history stays intact and Pro-only views switch off until you resubscribe.
Is the companion pay-to-win? No. Every stage and every piece of gear unlocks off logged training. None of it is purchasable.
Android? Not yet. BitRep is built on iOS 16+ and leans on Live Activities and widgets.
Who sees my data? Only you. No ad SDKs, no trackers, no selling — the privacy policy says exactly what is stored.
